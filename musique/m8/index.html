spacers and kill pattern

FE spacers FD Kill pattern

In the song view create An FE phrase and an FD phrase. Those are 4 empty bars used as spacers. FD is also a kill pattern (kill command on each FX).

4 bars to work with

Put 4 bars in the chains, they can be identical, but it’s easy to introduce some variations, if it is needed:

CHAIN 30

  PH TSP
0 31 00
1 32 00 // variation 1
2 31 00
3 34 00 // variation 2

Ça me parait une bonne idée de nommer les phrases en gardant les décimales qui correspondent aux chaînes, et en laissant la 0 de libre (peut-être une init phrase, un brouillon, une base, …)

Don’t start on a blank pattern

Clone

While selected: [SHIFT] + [EDIT]

Apply to:

  • chains
  • phrases

Copy the content of the selected chain/phrase into the next empty chain/phrase

Deep clone

While selected: [SHIFT] + [EDIT] [EDIT]

Apply globally to the selected chains and the phrases inside them. Copy the contents of the selected chain into the next empty chain and copy the contents of all its phrases into the next empty phrase(s).
